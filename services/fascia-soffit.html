Fascia & Soffit

Fascia & Soffit Repair in Winnipeg, the Trim Your Eavestrough Depends On

Fascia is the board along your roofline that eavestrough actually hangs from. Soffit is the panel underneath the overhang, usually vented to let attic air escape. When either one fails, it's rarely just cosmetic — it's the reason your eavestrough is sagging, or the reason ice is damming along your roof edge.

When you need fascia or soffit work

  • Fascia board is soft, rotting, or visibly stained below the eavestrough
  • Hangers are pulling out because the fascia underneath won't hold a fastener anymore
  • Soffit panels are missing, damaged, or blocking attic airflow
  • You're replacing your eavestrough and want the trim brought up to match
  • Squirrels, wasps, or birds have gotten into a gap

How our local pros handle it

  1. Inspect. Fascia and soffit checked for rot, water damage, and ventilation gaps along the whole run.
  2. Repair or replace. Damaged sections cut out and replaced with matching material, and soffit vents cleared or added where airflow is blocked.
  3. Reinstall eavestrough. Once the trim is solid, eavestrough is re-hung or installed fresh on top of it.
Blocked soffit vents are one of the most common, least-noticed contributors to ice damming. Without airflow, attic heat has nowhere to go but up through the roof, melting snow that refreezes at the eaves.
