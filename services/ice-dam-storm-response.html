Ice Dam & Storm Response in Winnipeg, When It Can’t Wait

Some eavestrough problems can sit on a to-do list for a month. Water backing up under your shingles isn't one of them. When ice damming or storm damage puts water where it doesn't belong, our matched pros prioritize the call and get eyes on it fast.

When you need priority response

  • Water is visibly backing up under your shingles or dripping inside the house
  • A summer storm has torn a section of eavestrough loose or filled it with debris
  • Heavy icicles or an ice ridge has formed along the roofline
  • A downspout has separated and is dumping water against the foundation
  • You need someone to assess damage quickly, even if the full fix has to wait for weather

How our local pros handle it

  1. Priority call-back. Urgent requests are flagged and matched first.
  2. On-site assessment. Your pro identifies whether it's an eavestrough issue, an attic ventilation issue, or both — see our fascia & soffit page for why that matters.
  3. Immediate stabilization. Temporary fixes to stop active water intrusion, with a full repair scheduled once conditions allow.
Ice dams are often treated as an eavestrough problem, but they usually start in the attic. A pro who checks both the eavestrough and the ventilation gives you a fix that actually holds through the next freeze-thaw cycle, not just a patch.
