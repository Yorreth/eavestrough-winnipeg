Eavestrough Installation

Eavestrough Installation in Winnipeg, Sized Right for Our Climate

Whether you're replacing rusted-out sectional gutters, finishing a new build, or dealing with a house that never had eavestrough at all, installation isn't a job where cutting corners pays off. Our matched pros form seamless aluminum eavestrough on-site and hang it with the slope and hanger spacing Winnipeg's freeze-thaw winters actually call for.

When you need new eavestrough

  • Your current eavestrough is sectional, rusted, or leaking at every seam
  • You're finishing a new build, addition, or re-roof
  • Your home has no eavestrough at all and water is running down the siding or pooling at the foundation
  • You're upsizing to handle a bigger roof or heavier rainfall
  • You want fascia and soffit replaced or repaired at the same time

How our local pros handle an installation

Every job starts with a free, no-obligation assessment of your roofline, fascia condition, and downspout placement. From there:

  1. Measure & assess. Roof size, pitch, corners, and existing fascia condition are all checked before quoting a price.
  2. Seamless forming, on-site. Aluminum coil is formed into one continuous length right at your home — no factory seams to fail later.
  3. Install & slope-check. Eavestrough is hung with proper pitch toward the downspouts and hangers spaced for our snow load, then water-tested before the crew leaves.
Winnipeg's freeze-thaw cycles put more stress on eavestrough hangers and seams than milder climates see. Pros working here typically use heavier-gauge aluminum and tighter hanger spacing than what's standard further south — worth asking about if you're comparing quotes.

What it costs

Most homes run roughly $6–$14 per linear foot for new seamless aluminum eavestrough installed, depending on fascia condition, corner count, and downspout placement. The only way to get an exact number is a quick, free assessment.

Common questions

Seamless eavestrough is formed on-site as one continuous piece per run, so there are no joints between corners — fewer places to leak, and a cleaner look. Sectional gutters are pre-cut lengths joined with sealant, which tends to fail over time.

Most homes are done in a single day, sometimes less for straightforward one-storey layouts. Larger or more complex rooflines can take longer.

Most seamless aluminum comes in a wide range of factory colours, and your matched pro can also handle fascia painting or replacement to match.
