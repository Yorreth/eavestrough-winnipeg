Repair & Resealing

Eavestrough Repair in Winnipeg, Fixed Before It Spreads

A leaking seam or a sagging section rarely stays a small problem for long. Water finds the weak spot and keeps working at it — into the fascia, down the siding, or pooling at the foundation. Our matched pros diagnose the actual cause before repairing, not just patch what's visible.

When you need repair

  • Water is dripping from a seam or joint instead of the downspout
  • A section is sagging, pulling away from the fascia, or missing hangers
  • Ice damming last winter left dents, splits, or pulled fasteners
  • A summer storm knocked a section loose or filled it with debris
  • The eavestrough is otherwise in good shape but a few problem spots need attention

How our local pros handle a repair

  1. Diagnose. The full run is checked for the source of the leak or sag, not just the visible symptom.
  2. Repair. Seams resealed, bent or corroded sections replaced, and problem areas re-hung and re-sloped where needed.
  3. Test. Water run through the repaired section to confirm it drains properly before the crew leaves.
A sagging section is rarely just cosmetic — standing water adds weight that stresses the fascia board it's hanging from. A repair that's put off tends to get more expensive, not less.
