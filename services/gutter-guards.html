Gutter Guards

Gutter Guards in Winnipeg, Fewer Cleanings and Less Ice-Dam Risk

If you're up on a ladder every few months clearing out the same trough, gutter guards are usually worth the investment — especially with mature trees on the property. They won't eliminate maintenance entirely, but they cut it down significantly and help keep debris from turning into a winter ice-dam problem.

When guards are worth it

  • You're cleaning your eavestrough more than twice a year
  • You have mature trees overhanging the roof
  • You don't want to be on a ladder every season
  • You're trying to reduce ice-damming caused by trapped debris and standing water
  • You're already replacing your eavestrough and want guards added at install

How our local pros handle it

  1. Assessment. Existing eavestrough condition and roof pitch checked, with a guard style recommended — mesh or hood (reverse-curve).
  2. Install. Guards fitted and secured along the full run, including corners and downspout openings.
  3. Final check. Water tested to confirm proper flow-through under the guard before the crew leaves.
No guard is fully maintenance-free — fine seeds and wind-blown grit can still collect on top of mesh guards. An annual rinse-off is still worth scheduling, especially before winter.
